Privacy Policy for Abhidhamma Matika

This application is designed for educational purposes only. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Feedback form (optional)

If you choose to send feedback, the information you type (message, and optionally your name and email address), together with basic technical details (language, screen size, browser/device type, page address) is sent to the developer through the EmailJS service so that the developer can read and reply to it. A copy is kept on your own device until it has been sent. You are never required to submit feedback.

Data stored on your device

Your theme preference (light/dark) and the offline copy of the app are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent anywhere.
